How we deliver

No black boxes. No surprise invoices.

Six phases, each with a clear output you sign off on — so budget, scope and quality stay visible from day one to launch and beyond.

Discovery & scoping

Workshops to pin down users, outcomes and constraints. You get a scoped backlog, risks and a fixed or ranged estimate.

Product design

User flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype tested with real users before a line of production code.

Architecture

Stack, data model, integrations and security decided up front — documented so any engineer can pick it up.

Agile build

Two-week sprints with a working demo every sprint. Shared board, shared repo, no black boxes.

QA & launch

Automated and manual testing, performance and security checks, then a staged rollout with monitoring.

Grow & support

Post-launch SLAs, analytics reviews and a roadmap for the next release — we stay until it's working.
