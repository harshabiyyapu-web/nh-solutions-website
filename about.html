Our story

We started by building for ourselves

NH Solutions started by building and growing its own digital properties — a publishing network that now reaches 3–5 million readers a month. Along the way we built the publishing tech, the SEO engine and the analytics to run it.

Then came a direct-to-consumer furniture brand we scaled 4× through performance marketing, and a multi-model AI workspace we built and run in-house. Each venture taught us something agencies rarely learn: what it feels like when the product is yours and the money is yours.

As the work grew, Harsha teamed up with Naman Seth in Delhi — a technologist with rare depth across architecture, backend systems and AI, and the operating discipline to run teams and execute a business plan week after week. Naman built the delivery engine that lets NH Solutions take on complex, high-stakes builds with confidence.

Today we put that operator mindset to work for founders, publishers and growing businesses — designing, engineering and growing products as if they were our own.
